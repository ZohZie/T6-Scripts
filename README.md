Timers: Top right Tital time and Round time, with Round Times being displayed and saved underneath

Velocity: Shows a nelocity reading at the middle bottom, changing colors for different speeds

bo4_round: moves the round counter to the top right

to switch controller bumpers and triggers, type this into the console, with the " ` "[FN+ESC] key:

/bind o "bind BUTTON_LSHLDR +speed_throw; bind BUTTON_RSHLDR +attack; bind BUTTON_LTRIG +smoke; bind BUTTON_RTRIG +frag"

then pressing "o" ingame each launch will switch the bumpers and triggers for that game session.
